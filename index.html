Examples

    Recent years look faster than they really are. Older CVEs have had years to collect late exploitation reports; recent ones have not yet. Measured over the same 90 days after publication, the median has been about zero days every year since 2021. What has grown is volume: roughly 130 CVEs a year were exploited within a week of disclosure in 2021–22, versus about 350 in 2024–25.
